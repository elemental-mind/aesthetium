Depth

Depth attributes control shadow elevation. Use them to make surfaces feel raised, floating, or intentionally flat without writing utility classes.

Single Element Depth

Apply depth directly when one element needs a specific elevation.

<article floating>Floating surface</article>
<article raised>Raised surface</article>
<article flat>Flat surface</article>
Floating surface
Raised surface
Flat surface

Attributes

AttributeEffect
[floating]Uses the larger shadow token for prominent elevated surfaces.
[raised]Uses the standard shadow token for subtle elevation.
[flat]Removes depth from the element.
[shadowless]Alias for [flat] when the intent is explicitly no shadow.

Parent Child Depth

Prefix the attribute with items- to apply depth to every direct child. A child can still override the parent treatment with its own plain depth attribute.

<div tiles items-floating>
  <article>Floating from parent</article>
  <article flat>Flat child override</article>
  <article raised>Raised child override</article>
</div>

Item Attributes

Parent AttributeApplies ToChild Override
[items-floating]Every direct child receives --shadow-lg.[raised], [flat], or [shadowless]
[items-raised]Every direct child receives --shadow.[floating], [flat], or [shadowless]
[items-flat]Every direct child receives no shadow.[floating] or [raised]
[items-shadowless]Alias for [items-flat].[floating] or [raised]
